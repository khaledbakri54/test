The Identity

A Mediterranean light, brought home.

RICCIOCAPRESE reads the profile of the riccio — the sea urchin, the emblem of Capri — as a lamp. Each one is thrown in clay and glazed by hand in the ancient Capodimonte tradition. This is the identity system for its arrival in Jordan.

Typography · two scripts, one voice
Display · Didone serif
Aa
A high-contrast Italian serif carries the heritage and the price point. Used large, with generous air, for headlines and product names.
Arabic · Naskh
ضوء
A classical Naskh partners the serif with equal weight — never a caption to it. The two scripts share the stage so the brand feels native in Amman and in Capri.
